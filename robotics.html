Robotics & Automation Solutions

I design, build and deploy custom robotic systems that streamline workflows, reduce bottlenecks and bring modern automation to small businesses and factories. Whether you need a single smart machine or an end‑to‑end upgrade, I’m here to help.

My approach starts with understanding your existing processes and pain points. From there, I design modular systems using off‑the‑shelf components, custom hardware and open‑source software so that you retain control and flexibility. I emphasise reliability, safety and scalability in every build.

Capabilities

  • Robotic arms for assembly, pick‑and‑place and inspection
  • Custom end effectors and grippers tailored to your products
  • Sensor integration (vision, force, proximity and IoT)
  • Programmable controllers and software interfaces
  • Retrofits to modernise legacy equipment

I believe that automation should empower people rather than replace them. My systems are designed to handle repetitive or hazardous tasks so your team can focus on creativity and quality. All solutions are documented and open where possible, so you’re never locked into proprietary code.
